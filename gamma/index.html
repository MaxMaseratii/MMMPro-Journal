What the levels mean

Call Wall — the strike with the biggest call positioning above price. Dealers hedging there often slow rallies down.

Put Wall — the strike with the biggest put positioning below price. Often a floor where selling gets absorbed.

Zero Gamma / GEX Flip — where dealer hedging changes from calming price to pushing it. Above it, moves tend to be calmer; below it, faster.

90D vs Today — 90D = the big picture over the next three months. Today = only the options that expire today (0DTE).
