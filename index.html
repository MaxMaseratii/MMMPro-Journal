What traders ask about MMM

What is MMM Trading?

MMM is the Max Maserati Model: an order-flow method for ES and NQ futures. It comes with TradingView indicators, a free interactive course, a prop-firm journal, and free daily gamma levels and a news calendar.

Who is MMM for?

Futures traders, mostly prop-firm traders on ES, NQ and their micros (MES, MNQ) who want clear rules and want to stay funded, not just get funded.

What does "order flow" mean in MMM?

Reading who is in control, buyers or sellers, from where each candle closes. A wick is a test; a close past a level is a vote. The MMM indicators mark where control changed hands (flips), where it kept going (continuations) and the zones the winners defend.

Is the MMM Mastery Course free?

Yes. It is free and interactive: 11 phases from reading one candle to a full entry checklist, with a quiz at the end of each phase and chart homework. You need a free account to save your progress.

What does the MMM Pro Journal do?

It tracks every prop account around a buffer you set and lock once: your risk per trade, a daily plan and mental check, a payout tracker, a discipline score and trade import from Tradovate, NinjaTrader, TopstepX, ProjectX and more.

Are the MMM strategies tested?

Yes. Every strategy we show is backtested after costs on NQ / MNQ from Dec 2022 to Oct 2026, over all the data and year by year. See the backtests and the strategy book.

Is this financial advice?

No. MMM is education and research. Futures trading carries a high risk of loss, and past results do not promise future results.
