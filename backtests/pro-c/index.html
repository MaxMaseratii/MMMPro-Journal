The short answer

  • The structure is real. A trend leg runs a median 5-6 ATR after the flip. Each next T# comes about half the time.
  • The gate is the key. A P# confirmed by the gate turns into a continuation 6 times in 10. Unconfirmed P#s almost never matter.
  • T-BRK flips are the strong flips: 67% reach T2 (57% without) and they run further.
  • No raw Pro c entry is an edge on its own. FLIP, LD / SD, PO4 retest, XU, U, RCONF and cdl / cds entries all trade close to random at 50 pts / 1:2, with or without HTF filters.
  • Best leads to watch live: Continuation XU on the 4h, and the 4h opening gap (5-20 pts) retest with the gap. Positive every year, not enough 2026 trades to prove it.
  • The alignment box tells the story but does not predict the next 1h or 4h. Keep the default settings: they give the cleanest structure.
  • Engine check: the Python copy matched the real Pine on TradingView (MNQ1! 1h, 1000 candles) flip for flip and level for level.

How to trade the opening gap (what the data says)

  • Do not just fade it. The fill comes 75-85% of the time, but the fill is small next to a 50 pt stop, so the fade made about 0 R per trade.
  • Better: trade WITH a 5-20 pt 4h gap. Wait until price moves away from the new open by the gap size (5 pts min), then buy / sell the first retest of the new open. Stop 50, target 3R. 154 trades, PF 1.61, positive every year. A lead, not a PASS yet.
  • Big Daily gaps (50+ pts) are the dangerous ones: only 6-7 in 10 fill and the fill can take a full day. Fading them at the open lost money.
  • The HTF Pro c trend side does not change the fill odds.
