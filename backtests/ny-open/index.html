09:30 to 11:30 New York

The two hours Max trades. What NQ does from the cash open to 11:30: when the high and low are made, what the 09:30 and 10:00 MMM candles tell you, the opening range, the 09:30–10:00 gaps, and how far a run of body closes goes before the other side closes. The window

Median 09:30–11:30 range: 176 pts. The first 15 minutes make the high on about 32% of days and the low on about 32%. The high or the low is already in by 10:00 on 83% of days, and on 80–86% in every year.

The 09:30 candle

A 5m BC+ at the open: the next 5m candle takes its target first 85% (81–90% each year), and the target is hit by 11:30 97%. But 11:30 only closes its way 54%: it shows the first push, not the morning.

The opening range

09:30–10:00 high to low: median 99 pts. Price closes out of it by 11:30 94% of days, then runs a median 53 pts past the edge. It closes back out of the OTHER side only 14%.

The 10:00 candle

A BC+ that closes at 10:00: the next candle takes its target first 87–90% on 5m, 15m, 1h and 4H. A BC− is close to a coin flip. A weak candle often visits the open gap behind it first.

Runs until 11:30

From the first BC after 10:00: median 2 same-side BCs, about 3–4 NBCs, then the other side closes. The run goes a median 32–38 pts (5m) and 39–41 pts (15m) our way, but the exit on the opposite candle gives it back.

What does NOT work

The 10:00 lean for the 16:00 close, a BC− for direction, holding until the opposite candle, and the first BC after 10:00 as an entry with a 50 pt stop at 1:2 (avg RR about 0, t-score under 2).

1 · When the high and the low are made09:30 to 11:30, by 15-minute slot

  • The open is where the extremes are made. The 09:30–09:45 slot makes the morning high or low on about 32% of days (29–34% each year), 3 to 4 times more than a middle slot.
  • The last slot (11:15–11:30) is second for the high (about 20% every year). Strong mornings often push into 11:30, so a long can be held until your stop time.
  • Both extremes after 10:00 is rare (17%, 14–20% each year). If 10:00 comes and price is far from the open, one side of the morning is probably already done.
  • Points grow every year because NQ price is higher (median range 130 pts in 2023, 265 in 2026). Compare the % between years, not the points.

2 · The 09:30 opening candleThe first 5m or 15m candle, read with MMM Candle Bias, until 11:30

Target (TGT) = the candle's lean-side extreme (high for a bull type). Protected = the other end. "11:30 its way" = the 11:30 close is past the 09:30 candle close in the lean direction. MFE = most points in favour from the candle close before 11:30, MAE = most points against.

  • C2 is the most common opening candle (about 40%), then BC+ (about 22%): the pre-open candle is small, so the open takes both sides of it.
  • BC+ is the cleanest: on 5m the next candle takes its target first 85% (81–90% each year), and the target comes before the protected end 91%.
  • None of them tells you the 11:30 close: about 50% (AFs only 30%). Use the 09:30 candle for the first push only.

3 · The 30-minute opening rangeThe 09:30–10:00 high and low, and the first close out of it before 11:30

  • The first close out of the range usually holds the morning: 11:30 still closes past that edge 57% (54–59% each year), and price closes back out of the other side only 14% (10–19%).
  • Up or down is about even (50% up, 44% down). Do not guess the side: wait for the first 1m close out of the range.

4 · The candle that closes at 10:004H 06:00–10:00 · 1h 09:00–10:00 · 15m 09:45–10:00 · 5m 09:55–10:00

  • BC+ works on every timeframe: the next candle takes the lean-side extreme first 87–90%. Every year stays above 84%, except the 4H in 2026 (76%, only 21 days). The close already sits next to that extreme, like a ball at the door.
  • BC− is a coin flip for which side goes first (about 50%). The close position matters more than the body close.
  • 16:00: the 10:00 lean does not tell you the close of the day (about 50–60%). Trade the morning, not the day.

5 · UPG gaps made 09:30–10:003-candle gaps on 5m and 1m: touch, fill, react or fail, until 16:00

Touch = price trades back into the gap. Full fill = through the far edge. React = after the touch, a 5m candle closes back out on the gap side before any 5m close past the far edge. Fail = the far-edge close comes first. The 4H 06–10 candle closes at 10:00, so before 10:00 you read it while it is still forming.

  • Opening gaps get touched fast: 5m gaps 82% (76–87% each year), median 5 minutes after they form. A gap is a sponge: price comes back to soak it up.
  • With the 4H 06–10 lean, a 5m gap reacts 68% (66–76% each year). Against it only 46% (35–57%). Trade the gaps that point the same way as the 4H candle.

6 · An open gap on the other side of the 10:00 targetDoes price visit the gap behind it before the target? 10:00 to 11:30

Gap = an untouched 5m UPG gap made since 18:00, fully on the other side of the 10:00 close (below price for a bull lean). The closest one counts. "Weak" = BC−, AFs, CI or S&D.

  • A gap is usually there (56–77% of days), and it does not stop the target.
  • Strong candles ignore it: after BC+ or C2, the gap comes first only 5% (5m) to 22% (1h).
  • Weak candles go there first 15% (5m), 30% (15m), 44% (1h), 50% (4H). The same in 2023, 2024 and 2025. On 1h and 4H, wait at the gap on those days.

7 · From the first BC after 10:00 until the opposite candle or 11:30Body closes our way, NBCs, points, minutes

The opposite candle: Opposite BC = the first body close the other way. Close past BC open = a close below the open of the last bull BC (bear run: above the last bear BC open). Close past BC low = a close below the low of the last bull BC (bear run: above its high).

  • Runs are short: median 2 same-side BCs on every chart. 1m runs end in about 5 minutes, 5m in 25–35, 15m in about an hour.
  • The points are there, the exit is late: MFE median 32–38 pts on 5m, but at the opposite candle the average is about 0, in every year (−5 to +5 pts). Take points before the other side closes.
  • "Close past BC low" holds the longest and gives the most points. The 4H lean does not change the run.

8 · 5m BC runs by the hour they startEvery run in the data, not only 10:00

  • 09:30–11:30 runs have the same count of BCs as other hours, but they travel more points: 29 pts median vs 7–19 for the other hours, and it is the top hour in every year. Same number of steps, bigger steps.

9 · 50 pt stop at 1:2 on the first BC after 10:00Entry at the BC close, stop 50, target 100, out at 11:30, 1 pt cost

WR = win rate. avg RR = average result in risk units (1 = one stop). PF = profit factor: money won ÷ money lost. t-score: 2 or more = probably not luck.

  • No edge. All data: avg RR +0.05 (5m) and +0.04 (15m), t-score under 2. Only one year group reaches t-score 2 (5m, first BC is a BC+, 2024), and 2026 is negative on 5m. The first BC after 10:00 is the start of the count, not an entry. The working morning strategies are in the strategy book.
