9:30 AM to 11:30 AM Testing Studies

Everything tested on the New York morning, the two hours Max trades: when the high and low are made, what the 09:30 and 10:00 MMM candles tell you, the opening range, the opening gaps, and how far a run of body closes goes before the other side closes. In one document, in plain words.

Updated 2026-11-04Market NQ / MNQ futuresData 993 days, 2022-12-27 → 2026-11-03, all data + each yearWindow 09:30–11:30 New York
The morning is made early

The high or the low of 09:30–11:30 is in by 10:00 on 83% of days (80–86% each year). The first 15 minutes make about a third of them.

BC+ points the way

A BC+ at 09:30 or at 10:00: the next candle takes its target first 83–90% of the time, on every timeframe, and above 80% in almost every year.

The opening range breaks once

Price closes out of 09:30–10:00 on 94% of days and runs a median 53 pts. It breaks both sides only 14%.

Gaps with the 4H side hold

09:30–10:00 gaps get touched in about 5 minutes. With the 4H candle they react 68%. Against it, 46%.

Runs are 2 BCs long

After 10:00 a run makes a median 2 body closes and 32–38 pts (5m) before the other side closes. Take points; the opposite candle gives them back.

No trade by itself

The first BC after 10:00 with a 50 pt stop at 1:2 has no edge (t-score under 2). These are maps of the morning, not entries.

The data and the rulesRead this first

  • All data = NQ / MNQ futures, 2022-12-27 to 2026-11-03, 993 trading days. Every day is counted once.
  • 2023 · 2024 · 2025 · 2026 = each year alone. 2023 starts on 2022-12-27. A few months of 2026 never weigh like 3 years.
  • Up to Dec 11 2025: NQ 1-minute data. After that: MNQ / NQ from TradingView and our data library, 1m where we have it (Mar–Apr and Sep–Nov 2026), else 5m (Jul–Sep) or 15m bars.
  • Each table only uses days with fine enough bars. Anything that looks inside a candle needs 1m bars: 836 days (2026: 73). * = fewer than 30 cases.
  • NQ price is about twice as high in 2026 as in 2023, so points grow every year. Compare the % between years, not the points.
  • All times are New York. Weekdays only. Days with a missing 1m bar between 09:30 and 09:59 were skipped.
  • The window ends at 11:30, the moment Max stops trading.
  • Candles are read with MMM Candle Bias: each candle compared with the one before it. TGT (target) = the lean-side extreme. Protected = the other end.
  • Parts 1 to 8 count what price does. They are not tested strategies. Part 9 is a real trade test with a 1 point cost.

Best read per timeframe

4H
  • A BC+ at 10:00: target first 88% (76% in 2026, only 21 days).
  • Weak 4H candle: the gap behind it comes first 50%. Wait there.
1h
  • BC+: target first 89% (85–92% each year).
  • Weak 1h candle: gap or target is a coin flip.
15m
  • The best balance. BC+: target first 90% (86–92% each year).
  • Runs after 10:00: about 6 in 10 still alive at 11:30.
  • 20 pts reached 73%, 30 pts 63%.
5m
  • The fastest. BC+: target first 87%.
  • Opening gaps react 68% with the 4H lean.
  • Runs end in 25–35 minutes. 20 pts reached 70%.

How to use it from 09:30 to 11:30A reading order built from these counts, not a tested strategy

  1. Before 09:30Note the 4H 06:00–10:00 candle forming and any open UPG gaps from the night. The 4H side is the filter for the opening gaps.
  2. 09:30–10:00Expect the morning high or low to be made here (about 1 day in 3 in the first 15 minutes). Read the 09:30 5m / 15m candle: a BC+ shows the first push. Mark the 09:30–10:00 range.
  3. 09:30–10:00 gapsA 5m gap that points WITH the 4H candle reacts 68% of the time. Against it, it fails more often than it holds.
  4. 10:00Read the candles that close at 10:00. BC+ → its target is likely next. BC− → no side. Weak 1h or 4H candle → price often visits the open gap behind it first.
  5. 10:00–11:30Count the run: about 2 BCs and 3–4 NBCs, then the other side closes. Take points (20–30 on 5m / 15m) before the opposite candle. Use "close past the BC low" as the line that ends the run.
  6. 11:30Stop. Most mornings already made their range, and the 10:00 lean says nothing about 16:00.
12

What does NOT work

  • The 10:00 lean to call the 16:00 close (about 50–60%).
  • A BC− candle for direction (coin flip).
  • Holding until the opposite candle: the run gives the points back (average about 0 at the exit).
  • The first BC after 10:00 as an entry with a 50 pt stop at 1:2 (no edge, t-score under 2).
  • Guessing the side of the opening range before the first close out of it (about 50/50).
13

Words used

BC
Body close: the candle closes past the previous candle’s high (bull) or low (bear). Urgency.
NBC
No body close: the candle closes inside the previous candle. Negotiation.
BC+ / BC−
A BC that closes in its strong quarter (+) or not (−).
C2
The candle takes the other side first, then closes through: an engulf.
AFs / CI / S&D
Sweep and close back inside / inside candle / takes both sides and closes inside.
TGT / protected
TGT = the lean-side extreme of the candle. Protected = the other end.
UPG gap
A 3-candle gap: candle 3 never comes back to candle 1’s high (bull).
MFE
The most points in our favour before the end.
Opening range
The high and low of 09:30–10:00.
