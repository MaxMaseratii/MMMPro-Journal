How a strategy gets into the book

  • Trade = it passed the MMM Testing Standard: it made money after costs in both test periods (Dec 2022 – Dec 2025, then 2026), with a t-score per day of 3 or more (the edge is very unlikely to be luck), and nothing was tuned on the 2026 data. These are the strategies inside MMM Pro Edge.
  • Every trade pays its costs, and limit orders fill the honest way: a candle that trades through your price fills you, even if it closes against you.
  • Backtest first = it made money in every year (WORKS), but its t-score per day is under 3 or one test period was weak, so it did not pass the Standard. It is profitable in our test: backtest it yourself before you use money. Watch = it made money but had one losing year or a weak t-score (MAYBE): paper-trade it first.
  • NO = no edge over all the data, or it lost money in two years or more. It does not go in the book.

      How to read the numbers

      WR
      Win rate: how often a trade ends in profit. A low WR is fine when the wins are much bigger than the losses.
      avg RR
      The average result of one trade, measured in stops. +0.20 means each trade made a fifth of its stop on average, after costs.
      PF
      Profit factor: all the money won divided by all the money lost. Above 1 makes money.
      t-score
      How sure we are it is not luck. The MMM Testing Standard uses the t-score per day: 3 or more to Trade. The year-by-year badge uses 2 or more over all trades. More trades and steadier results push it up.
      Year by year
      One report over all the data, then each year alone: 2023 (it also holds the last days of Dec 2022), 2024, 2025 and 2026 (up to Oct). Each year is judged on its own, so a few months never weigh like 3 years. A green year = it made money that year. Years with under 10 trades do not count.
      WORKS, MAYBE, NO
      WORKS = 100 trades or more, a t-score of 2 or more over all the data, and money made in every year that counts. MAYBE = it made money with a t-score of 1 or more, and at most one losing year. NO = anything else: no edge.
      Trade on
      The markets where the strategy works on that market’s own data. NQ only = it did not pass anywhere else yet.
      Trade, Backtest first, Watch
      Trade = passed the MMM Testing Standard: money in both test periods, t-score per day of 3 or more, no tuning on new data. These are in MMM Pro Edge. Backtest first = made money in every year but did not pass the Standard: backtest it yourself before using money. Watch = made money with one losing year or a weak t-score: paper-trade it first.
      Green, amber, red edge
      The colour on the left of each strategy card. Green = WORKS. Amber = MAYBE: it could be luck, or one year lost. Red = NO edge.
      Short data
      ES, gold and crude only have Dec 2025 to Oct 2026, about 11 months. They get one report and no years. Treat it as a first check, not proof.
      Equity curve
      The running total of every trade, in stops. A thin line marks the start of each new year.
      Example pictures
      Drawings of the setup, not real trades. Long shows the buy side, short the same setup upside down. Filter on draws the extra check the strategy needs.
      Live trading
      Expect less than the test. Slippage, missed fills and nerves cost money. Start small.

      Questions about the strategy book

      What is the MMM Strategy Book?

      Every day-trading strategy that passed our tests, written as steps you can follow on a chart: which chart, what time, where to enter, where the stop goes and when to get out, with a long and a short example picture.

      How does a strategy get into the book?

      It must make money after costs over all the data and hold up when each year is judged on its own. The badge on each strategy shows the verdict and the reason, and the results sheet shows every number.

      What do Trade, Backtest first and Watch mean?

      Trade = it passed the MMM Testing Standard: money in both test periods, a t-score per day of 3 or more, no tuning on new data. These 5 are inside MMM Pro Edge. Backtest first = it made money in every year but did not pass the Standard: backtest it yourself first. Watch = it made money with one losing year or a weak t-score: paper-trade it first.

      Which markets were tested?

      NQ / MNQ from Dec 2022 to Oct 2026, where the rules were built. ES / MES, gold and crude oil from Dec 2025 to Oct 2026, marked as short data.

      Are trading costs included?

      Yes. Every trade pays its costs, and limit orders fill the honest way: a candle has to trade through your price.

      How should I use it?

      Start with one strategy. Study it, check that it fits your style and backtest it yourself before you put money on it. If you run several, run them as tested: one trade at a time with a daily rule.
