How a strategy gets into the book

  • It makes money after costs on every trade.
  • Its t-score is 2 or more, so it is probably not luck.
  • It still made money on the fresh 2026 data: the 2-period test.
  • Limit orders fill the honest way: a candle that trades through your price fills you, even if it closes against you.
  • Too few fresh trades, or a weak fresh result? It stays in the book as Watch. Paper-trade those first.

      How to read the numbers

      WR
      Win rate: how often a trade ends in profit. A low WR is fine when the wins are much bigger than the losses.
      avg RR
      The average result of one trade, measured in stops. +0.20 means each trade made a fifth of its stop on average, after costs.
      PF
      Profit factor: all the money won divided by all the money lost. Above 1 makes money.
      t-score
      How sure we are it is not luck. 2 or more is the bar. More trades and steadier results push it up.
      2-period test
      Period 1 = Dec 2022 – Dec 2025 (NQ, where the rules were found). Period 2 = Dec 2025 – Oct 2026 (fresh MNQ). PASS = avg RR above +0.05 in both. It is a test, not a profit score.
      Trade and Watch
      Trade = proven in both periods. Watch = it passed, but period 2 has few trades or a weak result. Paper-trade a Watch strategy before using money.
      Green, yellow edge
      The colour on the left of each strategy card. Green = made money and t-score 2+. Yellow = made money but the t-score is under 2: it could be luck.
      Equity curve
      The running total of every trade, in stops. The shaded part on the right is period 2, the fresh data.
      Example pictures
      Drawings of the setup, not real trades. Long shows the buy side, short the same setup upside down. Filter on draws the extra check the strategy needs.
      Live trading
      Expect less than the test. Slippage, missed fills and nerves cost money. Start small.
