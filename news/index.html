What it does on your chart

        
    
          
  • A dot on the candle 15 or 30 minutes before the news. Red = high, orange = medium, yellow = low, grey = holiday.
    • 
          
  • A tiny name next to it (CPI, FOMC, NFP…). Hover the dot to see the time.
    • 
          
  • A shaded window from 5 minutes before to 15 minutes after the news. You can change both.
    • 
          
  • A News Warning alert you can turn on in TradingView.
    • 
          
  • The dot, the name and the shade go away when price closes past the dot.
    • 
          
  • It does not stop signals or trades. It warns you. You decide.
    •
