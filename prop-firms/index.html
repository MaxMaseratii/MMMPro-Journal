    Copier groups: trade these together

    A trade copier sends one trade to many accounts. It only works well when every account has the same payout rule. These groups put together the plans that let you copy your own accounts and share the same funded consistency rule.

    Copy inside one groupMix a 50% plan with a 35% plan and one big day can block the 35% payout. Stay in one group.
    Smallest account leadsMake the account with the smallest drawdown the leader. Size the others by drawdown, not by account size.
    Same direction onlyEvery firm here bans hedging between your own accounts. Never copy one account long and another short.

    Words on this page

    EOD drawdown
    The floor moves up only with your closing balance. Firms still check it live: an open loss that touches the floor fails the account.
    Static drawdown
    The floor never moves. The safest kind, usually with a smaller drawdown.
    Intraday trailing
    The floor follows your best open profit, even inside a trade. Left off this list.
    Activation fee
    A second payment when you pass, before you can trade funded. Left off this list.
    Consistency rule
    No single day may be more than a set % of your total profit (for example 40%).
    Daily loss limit
    Soft or pause = your day stops. Hard = the account fails.
    1st payout
    The fewest funded trading days before you can ask for money. Day 1 = you can ask after your first funded day.
    Trade copier
    Software that copies one trade to all your accounts at once.
    Trustpilot hidden
    Trustpilot hides a firm's score when it finds fake or rule-breaking reviews. Read the reviews with care.
