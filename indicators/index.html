The philosophy

Price only moves when one side runs out of the other side's orders

Every trade needs a buyer and a seller. What matters is who is in a hurry. The urgent side sends market orders and takes what is on offer. The patient side waits with limit orders. MMM reads that fight from one simple thing: where each candle closes.

01

Demand meets offer

Buyers in a hurry lift the sell offers above price. When the last offer at a price is eaten, the next one is higher, so price ticks up. When both sides are equally in a hurry, price swings in a range: balance. When one side gives up, price leaves the range: imbalance.

02

A wick is a test, a close is a vote

A wick means price visited a level and the other side pushed it back: the orders there were tested, not beaten. A body close beyond the last candle means the market accepted the new price. The defenders were absorbed or gave up.

03

Most closes are noise

A few closes happen exactly where control was being defended: the origin of the last push, the edge of a balance zone, a swing, a fair value area. Finding those closes, and the zone each one leaves behind, is what the MMM engines do.

The foundation · how to read every candle

MMM Candle Bias Pro

Before any structure, MMM reads the candle. Each closed candle is compared with the candle before it: did it close above its high, below its low, or back inside after a sweep? H / L lines + TGTThe high and low of the last closed HTF candle, starting at the exact candle that made them. The lean side is the target (green dashed, labelled TGT). The other side is protected (red solid): the end the candle's owners must defend. Neutral candles keep grey lines.
BuBC+ H1H TGTEvery label tells you three things: the candle type, the timeframe and the level. Read it as: "the last H1 candle was a strong bull body close, its high is the target".
O LiveThe open of the HTF candle that is forming now. Price above it = the live candle is bullish so far, below it = bearish.
Bias EngineOptional fill between price and the HTF open (or last close, or pivot). Green fill = price is above it, purple = below. The table shows "Trend Bias: Bullish / Bearish" and can alert you when it changes.
Trapped Sellers · Buyers AbsorbedSweep and absorption: a candle with a volume spike and a big wick takes an MMCB high or low and closes back. The box marks where the trapped side got stuck. It is deleted when price closes past the sweep's extreme.
Timer tableYour local time and New York time, a countdown to the close of each timeframe (1m to 4H), each row coloured by the bias of its last closed candle, a highlight for your main timeframe and a red warning in the last 30 seconds.
AlertsWhen an HTF candle bias forms, when a sweep traps one side, and when the trend bias changes. Only on closed candles.
