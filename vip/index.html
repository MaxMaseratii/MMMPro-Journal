Questions

What traders ask before they join

Is V.I.P really free?

Yes. Finish the 5 steps above and your code shows on screen. You can also get a code from Max directly.

Where do the numbers come from?

One master trade file: every MMM Pro Edge trade on NQ and MNQ from Dec 26 2022 to Oct 16 2026, with real entry and exit times. A strategy is kept only if it made money in both test periods with a t-score per day of 3 or more, without tuning on the new data. The study page shows the method trader by trader.

Which prop accounts does it work for?

EOD trailing and static drawdown accounts. Type your firm's real rules: drawdown, target, daily limit, payout rules. Intraday (unrealized) trailing drawdown is not tested and the tools tell you to avoid it.

Do I need the indicator?

The tools plan around MMM Pro Edge's 5 strategies. You can also type your own strategy's stats or paste your own trades in the Prop Scale Planner.

What are the limits?

It is a backtest. Real trading is usually worse: slippage, missed trades, nerves. The planner cuts the edge a little on purpose, and every page says how the test works. It is research, not financial advice.

Can I lose access?

Codes can be switched off if they are shared in public. One code per email, and it works on all your devices.
