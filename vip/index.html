The study behind V.I.P

We backtested the strategies of the traders who get paid millions.

We studied 171 interviews of futures prop traders who claim $250k to $5M in payouts, from 6 popular YouTube channels. We wrote down their rules in their own words, coded 57 of their strategies and backtested each one year by year on NQ, ES and gold.

Only 4 of their 48 headline rules made money year after year. Yet their payouts are real. Their edge is mostly how they run their accounts: many accounts, big risk to pass, small risk once funded, payouts as soon as the rules allow. V.I.P turns that method into numbers for your own account.

Questions

What traders ask before they join

How do MMM users get V.I.P access?

Support the work in 5 steps: share the backtested study on X, boost 5 MMM indicators on TradingView, follow on TradingView, follow on X and subscribe on YouTube. Then type your email and your personal code shows on screen. You can also get a code from Max directly.

What is the backtested study?

We studied 171 interviews of futures prop traders who claim $250k to $5M in payouts, from Chart Fanatics, Titans of Tomorrow, Words of Rizdom, Speculators, Desire To Trade and Chat With Traders. We coded 57 of their strategies from their own words and backtested them year by year on NQ, ES and gold. Only 4 of their 48 headline rules made money year after year: their payouts come mostly from how they run their accounts.

Where do the V.I.P numbers come from?

One master trade file: every MMM Pro Edge trade on NQ and MNQ from Dec 26 2022 to Oct 16 2026, with real entry and exit times. A strategy is kept only if it made money in both test periods with a t-score per day of 3 or more, without tuning on the new data.

Which prop accounts does it work for?

EOD trailing and static drawdown accounts. Type your firm's real rules: drawdown, target, daily limit, payout rules. Intraday (unrealized) trailing drawdown is not tested and the tools tell you to avoid it.

Do I need MMM Pro Edge?

MMM Pro Edge comes inside the MMM PRO package. The Game Plan Builder plans around its 5 strategies. In the Prop Scale Planner you can also type your own strategy's stats or paste your own trades.

What are the limits?

It is a backtest. Real trading is usually worse: slippage, missed trades, nerves. The planner cuts the edge a little on purpose, and every page says how the test works. It is research, not financial advice.

Can I lose access?

Codes can be switched off if they are shared in public. One code per email, and it works on all your devices: open "Already a V.I.P member?" under Free V.I.P access.
